Welcome

This walks through the handful of real things the panel needs to know about where you are: your location, your home airport (for the flight tracker and departure board), a window bearing (for the plane-in-window takeover), and a team to follow. Every step is optional and skippable — you can always change any of this later from the control panel.

Where is the panel?

Drives the ISS/satellite tracker, the flight radar's range rings, and sunrise/sunset on the clock. Real GPS from this device, or enter coordinates by hand.

Home airport (optional)

Marks the airport on the flight radar and powers the departure board (real local traffic classified as departing from/arriving at this airport). There's no free airport lookup this project could verify works reliably, so the coordinates are a one-time manual entry — search "[code] airport coordinates" if you don't have them handy.

Window bearing (optional)

The bearing your favorite window faces, so the flight tracker can flag a real aircraft actually visible out it. Stand at the window facing straight out and tap Capture — this uses your phone's real compass (a permission prompt may appear once). No compass? Enter the bearing by hand (0° = north, clockwise).

A team to follow (optional)

Gets a full-screen score with live game state whenever they're playing. You can add more later, or set up a whole city's teams at once, from the control panel's Sports card.

All set

Everything above can be changed any time from the control panel — favorite aircraft, night dimming, follow-a-flight, the events log, and every other mode all live there too.
