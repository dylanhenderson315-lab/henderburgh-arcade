Home location — ISS, flights & weather

One shared location for all three modes — ISS pass predictions, nearby aircraft and the NWS weather gridpoint. Weather is US-only (NWS coverage).

Follow a flight — anywhere, by callsign

Type the ICAO radio callsign: airline three letters + number. UAL123 is United. UA123 is not — that is the IATA ticket code and will read NOT AIRBORNE even if the plane is up. Same trap: DAL not DL, SWA not WN, AAL not AA. The phone shows LOOKING / NO SIGNAL / NOT AIRBORNE as three different facts. Ambient only dwells when it is actually airborne.

Favorite aircraft — watched tail numbers

A favorite aircraft is flagged brightest on the radar scope and in THE HANGAR — it outranks even a heavy or a helicopter on its own. Registration only (a tail number), the same form THE HANGAR lists entries by.

Now Playing — Last.fm

Free key, no OAuth: last.fm/api/account/create. Works with anything that scrobbles to Last.fm — Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, YouTube Music. The saved key is never shown back here once set (same as the Home Assistant token) — leaving the API key field blank and saving only changes the username, it won't clear an existing key.
One language: bass mass in the center, air on the wings, peak dots that linger. Palettes are the finish — vinyl warm, spectrum, studio cool, furniture mono. Left/right on the panel walks them. Quiet is an analog clock chip. Never a fake 24-band FFT. Gain is how hard it hits. Bass weight is how heavy the low mass feels.

Ambient — director

AUTO locks a live favorite game in the in-depth view. WORLD is data only. ARCADE is self-playing games. MIX is your list. Up/down on the pad also switches channel.

Mix — what you want

Saved mix is used only on the MIX channel. AUTO and WORLD ignore this list. A live game still opens in DETAIL whenever sports is showing.

Sports — leagues & pinned team

ESPN's unofficial API is for this house. A sold unit stays OFF until a licensed source exists.
Ctrl/Cmd-click (or tap each) to select more than one. More leagues = more requests against ESPN's undocumented API.
The pinned team gets a full-screen score with live game state.

Favorite teams — ticker watchlist

A cross-sport watchlist, separate from the one pinned team above — when the filter is on, the rotating ticker only shows games involving a team on this list. "Add all" matches every real team (across every league) whose real home city contains what you typed, e.g. "Pittsburgh" adds the Steelers, Penguins, and Pirates in one click.

Golf — pinned player

Surname is enough — accents are handled. Their position, score to par and hole are shown full-screen, and the panel flashes on a notable move (eagle, birdie, bogey, taking or losing the lead).

Tennis — pinned player

Surname is enough — accents are handled. Their opponent and real set-by-set score are shown full-screen, leading with whichever real ATP/WTA match they're currently in.

News — RSS source

Any standard RSS 2.0 feed. Headlines only — never article bodies. AP's and Reuters' public URLs currently redirect to their homepages with nothing parseable, so they aren't offered as presets.

Guestbook — source

Visitor messages posted to the HENDERBURGH site. This is a guestbook / message board, not an article feed.
