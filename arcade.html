Home location — ISS, flights & weather

One shared location for all three modes — ISS pass predictions, nearby aircraft and the NWS weather gridpoint. Weather is US-only (NWS coverage).

Sports — leagues & pinned team

The pinned team gets a full-screen score with live game state. More leagues = more requests against ESPN's undocumented API.

Golf — pinned player

Surname is enough — accents are handled. Their position, score to par and hole are shown full-screen, and the panel flashes on a notable move (eagle, birdie, bogey, taking or losing the lead).

Tennis — pinned player

Surname is enough — accents are handled. Their opponent and real set-by-set score are shown full-screen, leading with whichever real ATP/WTA match they're currently in.

News — RSS source

Any standard RSS 2.0 feed. Headlines only — never article bodies. AP's and Reuters' public URLs currently redirect to their homepages with nothing parseable, so they aren't offered as presets.

Guestbook — source

Visitor messages posted to the HENDERBURGH site. This is a guestbook / message board, not an article feed.
